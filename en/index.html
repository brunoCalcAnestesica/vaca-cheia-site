Vaca Cheia

Ready to fill the cow? An app to organize cost splits (informal pools) among people you know: goal, participants, who reported payment, and who the organizer confirmed — without a spreadsheet or chasing everyone in the group chat.

What the app does NOT do: it does not receive, hold, split, retain, or intermediate money; there is no wallet, balance, split payout, or fee. Pix or bank transfer happens outside the app, to the organizer’s key or link. There is no public pool discovery. For adults 18+ only, with mandatory acceptance. In store version 1.0: no in-app purchases and no ads.

What the app does

  • Create and edit pools with goal, deadline, optional fixed amount, and organizer Pix.
  • List participants by nickname; edit amounts; pending, reported, or confirmed status; filters in “Who paid”.
  • Show totals, goal percent, and amount left.
  • Share invite with message preview and nudge for pending payers; optional local reminder.
  • Close, reopen, or duplicate a pool; text/CSV report with copy.
  • Onboarding tutorial; erase all data on device.

Your data stays on your device. Read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, or contact support.

Vaca Cheia is an organization tool among people who know each other. It does not process payments or guarantee anyone paid. For adults 18+ only. App owner: MEDCALK LTDA. Contact: vacacheia@meninodaroz.org.
